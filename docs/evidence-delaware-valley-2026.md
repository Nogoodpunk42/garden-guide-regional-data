# Philadelphia/Delaware Valley pilot evidence

Reviewed: August 28, 2026

## Scope

This first non-New Jersey pack intentionally reviews only 45 of the universal catalog's 255 plant
identities. It publishes 46 unique recommendation records because lettuce maps to both leaf and
Bibb lettuce. Every plant outside this reviewed set retains the fail-closed `unreviewed` status.

## Vegetable evidence

Twenty-six vegetable identities use University of Delaware Cooperative Extension home-garden
guidance:

- [Planning a Vegetable Garden](https://www.udel.edu/academics/colleges/canr/cooperative-extension/fact-sheets/planning-a-vegetable-garden/)
  provides Delaware planting dates for seeds and transplants, including spring and fall successions.
- [Grow Your Own Food](https://www.udel.edu/academics/colleges/canr/cooperative-extension/environmental-stewardship/lawn-and-garden/grow-your-own/)
  explicitly supports April–May sowing for arugula and collards in community and home gardens.

Month arrays are conservative calendar-month projections of the extension's published date ranges.
They do not claim that every day in a listed month is suitable; weather, soil temperature, cultivar,
and the property's last-frost date remain controlling factors.

## Native landscape evidence

Nineteen landscape identities appear in the Philadelphia Water Department's
[Native and Recommended Non-Invasive Plants](https://water.phila.gov/wp-content/uploads/files/table-i-1-non-invasive-plants-1.pdf)
table. The source identifies Philadelphia County or Pennsylvania nativity and supplies site factors
such as moisture, light, drought tolerance, mature size, and commercial availability.

The pack uses nursery-grown installation in the conventional spring and fall establishment windows.
Individual site moisture and light requirements in the Philadelphia table still control selection;
regional eligibility is not a promise that every listed plant fits every yard location.

## Routing

The signed manifest assigns complete ZIP prefixes 190–199 to this pilot. Its coordinate-only
fallback covers the area west of the South Jersey boundary and intentionally does not guess across
the Delaware River. The pack requires Garden Guide version code 47 (`v0.13.0`) because that release
generates region controls from the signed catalog.
