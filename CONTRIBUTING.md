# Contributing regional evidence

Regional packs are reviewed evidence snapshots, not crowdsourced planting opinions.

Every recommendation-grade review must include:

- a universal `plantId`;
- `recommended` or `conditional` status;
- a review date;
- an HTTPS source from an extension service, government agency, university, or comparable primary authority;
- an explicit planting action;
- at least one planting month;
- the exact recommendation key or keys it enables;
- a permanent condition when the status is conditional.

`reference_only` and `prohibited` decisions must explain their restriction. Missing plants inherit
the region's `unreviewed` default and cannot enter automatic recommendations.

Review changes should be small enough for a person to audit. Do not silently broaden one crop's
evidence to a botanical relative, and do not copy regional timing solely because two locations share
a USDA hardiness zone.
